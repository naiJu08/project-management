## 📝 How to Use

1. **Click Share Button** - Opens the modal
2. **Copy Link** - Click "Copy" to share link
3. **Share with Team** - Enter email, select role, click "Share"
4. **Manage Permissions** - Toggle options as needed
5. **View Access** - See all shared users in the list
6. **Remove Access** - Click delete button on user
7. **Export** - Click PDF, Excel, or JSON to export
8. **Close** - Click X or backdrop to close
